v1.0 — Web Flasher
Flash Instructions
1
Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
Web flashing uses WebSerial — Firefox does not support it.
2
Plug your ESP32 into your computer via USB.
Close Arduino IDE or any serial monitor first.
3
Click CONNECT below.
Select your ESP32 COM port when the browser prompts you.
4
Wait ~30 seconds for flashing to complete,
then unplug and replug your ESP32.
⚠ WebSerial not supported. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge.
Chrome / Edge only  ·  ESP32 required
